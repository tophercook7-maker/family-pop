How to Play

🖼️ Look at the picture(s) and figure out the word.

🔤 Tap letters to spell it. Tap a placed letter to take it back.

➕ Some puzzles combine two pictures, some are riddles, some are two words.

💡 Hints cost coins. Solve with no hints for 3⭐ and combo bonuses — then spend coins in the 🛍️ Shop!
